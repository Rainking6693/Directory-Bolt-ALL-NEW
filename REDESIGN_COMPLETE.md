### ✅ Volt Restraint
- Volt used <10% of viewport
- Volt only for: CTAs, focus rings, active states, 1-2 hero metrics
- NO Volt for: Headings, body text, decorative borders

### ✅ Typography
- All headings use `text-role-text-primary` (neutral)
- Inter/Inter Display for headings
- Inter for body text
- JetBrains Mono for numbers/metrics
- Source Serif 4 for certificate moments only

- All components use role tokens consistently
